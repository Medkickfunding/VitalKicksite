How a selfie becomes your vital signs.

Every heartbeat changes the colour of your skin by a tiny amount. VitalKick's camera engine reads that change for 60 seconds, on your phone, and turns it into twelve readings. Here is how, and what we built around it.

01 · The signal

Your pulse is visible to a camera.

With each beat, a little more blood flows into the skin of your face. Blood absorbs green light strongly, so your skin turns very slightly darker and lighter in time with your heart.

The change is far too small for your eyes. A phone camera records 30 or more frames a second, and averaging thousands of skin pixels per frame brings the pulse out of the noise. The method is called remote photoplethysmography, or rPPG: measuring blood flow with light, without touching you.

02 · Step by step

What happens during the scan.

STEP 1

Find the skin

Each frame, the engine locates your forehead and cheeks, where blood flow shows best. It uses their position in that frame only and never stores the shape of your face.

STEP 2

Read the colour

It averages the colour of those skin areas, frame after frame, building a signal that rises and falls with every heartbeat.

STEP 3

Check the quality

Light, movement and talking all add noise. Every reading carries a confidence score, and a poor scan is flagged so you can try again in better light.

STEP 4

Compute the readings

From the clean pulse wave the engine works out your pulse, the timing between beats, breathing, oxygen and blood pressure, plus two clearly labelled estimates.

03 · Why 60 seconds

Each reading needs its own amount of signal.

A pulse rate is ready early. Heart rate variability needs about 50 seconds of heartbeats before the number means anything physiologically. One full minute gives every reading enough data, from a single scan.

05 · Built around the engine

What VitalKick adds on top of the signal.

ENGINE-NEUTRAL

Not tied to one supplier

VitalKick licenses its rPPG engine through a single internal layer. The rest of the app never talks to the engine directly, so a qualified alternative can be switched in without rebuilding the product.

CITED RANGES

Every range names its source

Normal ranges live in data, not in code, and each one cites the published source it came from. A physician can change a range without an app release.

RULES FIRST

Rules decide what to flag

Fixed, physician-reviewed rules choose the two or three numbers worth your attention. AI may only reword an approved message more warmly, and its wording is checked before you see it.

ALWAYS ON

A same-day prompt that cannot be hidden

If one reading crosses an urgent level, a calm prompt to get checked today appears before anything else. No setting, paywall or dismissed state can turn it off.
