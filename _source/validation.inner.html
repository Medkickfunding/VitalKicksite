How the engine inside VitalKick was tested.

VitalKick licenses its rPPG engine, the software that reads vital signs from video of the face. This page summarises how our technology partner tested that engine: who took part, which devices it was compared against, and what happened in real-world pilots.

How a scan works

The phone's front camera records the face for 60 seconds. The engine finds the forehead and cheeks, follows the tiny colour changes each heartbeat causes in the skin, and turns them into a pulse wave. Everything runs on the phone, and the video is never uploaded.

ReadingTime needed
Pulse rate20 seconds
Blood pressure, oxygen saturation35 seconds
Breathing rate, pulse-respiration quotient37 seconds
Heart rate variability, stress level, sympathetic and parasympathetic activity50 seconds

Heart rate variability needs about 50 seconds of heartbeats before the numbers mean anything physiologically, which is why a full scan lasts one minute.

Who it was tested on

  • Fitzpatrick skin types II to VI
  • Body mass index from 19 to 48
  • Women and men
  • Multiple ethnicities
  • People living with a wide range of health conditions
  • Lab conditions and everyday conditions

What it was compared against

Each lab measurement was taken alongside an established reference device. Sessions ran in a testing room with fixed artificial light, managed by clinicians who checked that conditions, setup and participants met the rules set for each vital sign.

Blood pressureWithings BPM Connect arm cuff
Breathing rateVernier Go Direct respiration belt
Hemoglobin and HbA1c estimatesLab blood draws

What this means, and what it does not

  • These results describe our technology partner's testing of the engine. They are not a medical claim about any single reading.
  • VitalKick is a general wellness product. Readings support your own wellness and do not replace care from your clinician.
  • Hemoglobin and HbA1c are estimates and are labelled that way in the app. Only a blood test can confirm them.
  • Readings work best in good, even light with the face still. Movement, talking and strong backlight lower signal quality.
