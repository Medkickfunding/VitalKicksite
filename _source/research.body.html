How matching works

From protocol criteria to a referred participant in four steps.

01 · DEFINE

You set the population

Age, region, scan-based ranges (for example average systolic BP over two weeks) and self-reported details such as medications or conditions.

02 · MATCH

We search consented users

Only people who opted in to research contact are searched. Repeated scans are averaged, so one odd reading does not decide anything.

03 · INVITE

They choose to take part

Matched users get a plain-language invitation and give study-specific consent in the app. Nobody is shared with you before that.

04 · REFER

Your site confirms

Consenting participants are referred to your site, which runs the protocol's own screening: cuff BP, lab A1c, physician review.

Elevated blood pressure pilot

Who
Adults 45–70
Signal

Then

Who
Adults 35–65, BMI 25 or above from self-reported height and weight
Signal
HbA1c estimate of 5.7% or above, flagged as an estimate
Then
Lab A1c at the site decides eligibility
Threshold: American Diabetes Association, A1C 5.7–6.4%

Guardrails

What VitalKick does, and what it never does.

Consent comes first

Only people who opted in to research contact are searched, and each study needs its own consent. Withdrawing is one tap.

No identification by face

VitalKick stores no face images or face geometry. Matching uses scan readings and what people tell us, never facial recognition.

No eligibility calls from a camera

Readings are wellness signals. Your site confirms every criterion with its own instruments before anyone is enrolled.

Aggregate counts only

Before consent, sponsors see de-identified counts, with any group under 20 suppressed. Never names, never individual readings.

Your protocol, your IRB

Recruitment runs under your IRB-approved protocol and materials, with a business associate agreement where health data is involved.

Video never leaves the phone

The camera signal is processed on the device. Only the computed numbers are stored, and every access is logged.
