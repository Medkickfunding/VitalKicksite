How it works

A pulse, read from light.

  1. 01
    Blood changes the colour of skin

    Each heartbeat pushes a little more blood into the small vessels of the face, and blood absorbs green light.

  2. 02
    The camera records the change

    The forehead and cheeks get very slightly darker and lighter with every beat, far too little for the eye to see.

  3. 03
    The phone turns it into readings

    On the device, the signal becomes a pulse wave and then twelve readings. The video is discarded when the scan ends.

Privacy by construction

Rules written into the product.

Enforced in code and in the database, not left to policy.

Video
Processed on the device and never uploaded, logged or saved unencrypted.
Face data
No face images and no face geometry are stored. VitalKick cannot recognise who you are from your face.
Consent
Every health-data write checks consent in the database. Withdrawing is recorded as a new entry, never by editing history.
Access log
Every read and write of health data records who, what, when and from where.
