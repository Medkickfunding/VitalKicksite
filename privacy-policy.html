Privacy policy

How VitalKick handles information on this website and in the app. The camera video never leaves your phone, and your health data is never sold.

The short version

  • The camera video never leaves your phone. We never store face images or face geometry.
  • We keep the numbers your scan produces, and what you choose to log, so you can see your trends.
  • We never sell identifiable health data, and never sell, lease or profit from anything biometric.
  • Research use is a separate, optional consent. Saying no changes nothing about the app.
  • You can export, delete and see who accessed your data at any time.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers.

01Who we are and what this covers

VitalKick ("VitalKick", "we", "us") makes a general wellness app that reads vital signs from a 60-second selfie video. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you can make. It covers:

  • this website, including the booking and pilot request forms
  • the VitalKick app for iPhone and Android, when you sign up for it yourself

When your clinician, health plan or another healthcare organisation enrols you in VitalKick, we handle your information on their behalf as their business associate under HIPAA. In that case their Notice of Privacy Practices governs your health information, and we follow our business associate agreement with them. The parts of this policy about the scan, security and biometric information still apply.

On this website

  • Hosting logs. Our host, Vercel, records standard details for each visit, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page opens.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising pixels or session recording.

What we never collect

We never upload, store or share the camera video, still images of your face, face geometry, face templates or any other face data. We do not use facial recognition. Please do not send patient information through the website forms or by text.

03How the scan handles your camera

The rPPG engine runs on your phone. During the scan, camera frames are held in the phone's memory only long enough to measure the tiny colour changes each heartbeat causes in your skin. When the scan ends, the frames are discarded. Only the computed numbers are saved to your account. This is enforced in the app's code, not just promised here.

04How we use information

  • To run the scan, show your results against published ranges, and draw your trends
  • To pick the two or three numbers worth your attention and suggest everyday habits, using rules reviewed by a physician
  • To show a same-day prompt when a reading crosses an urgent level
  • To share readings with an organisation, only when you join its program and agree in the app
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and improve it
  • To reply to bookings and pilot requests
  • To send product news, only if you opted in to marketing
  • To meet legal duties

We do not use your health information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

06How we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • With you, and with people you choose, such as a family member you invite.
  • With organisations you join. If you join a program run by your clinician, employer, health plan or a study, we share the readings that program needs, after you agree in the app.
  • With service providers who host our systems or help us run the service, under written contracts that limit their use of the data. Providers that handle health data sign a business associate agreement or an equivalent agreement.
  • For research, de-identified only, and only if you opted in. See the next section.
  • For legal reasons, when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our rights.
  • In a sale or merger of VitalKick, where the buyer must keep honouring this policy.

We never sell identifiable consumer health data. We never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric identifiers or biometric information.

07De-identified research

If you opt in to research, your data can be included in research datasets after everything that could identify you is removed. We follow the HIPAA Safe Harbor standard: all 18 listed identifiers are removed, dates are reduced to the year, ages over 89 are grouped together, and locations are no finer than the first three digits of a ZIP code.

  • Every dataset puts each person in a group of at least 20 people who look the same in the data.
  • Anyone receiving the data is bound by contract not to re-identify anyone or combine it with other data to do so. We make the same commitment.
  • Data from programs run under HIPAA and data from people who signed up directly are never mixed in a research dataset.
  • If you withdraw, your data stops being included in new datasets within 24 hours. Data already delivered in a de-identified dataset cannot be recalled, because it no longer carries anything that links it to you.

Separately, for study recruitment, we search only people who opted in to research contact. Sponsors see counts only, with groups under 20 suppressed, until a person gives study-specific consent.

08Biometric information: retention and destruction

This section is our public retention and destruction policy for biometric identifiers and biometric information, as Illinois law (740 ILCS 14/15(a)) requires.

  • VitalKick does not collect, store or keep biometric identifiers such as a scan of face geometry, and does not create face templates or face embeddings.
  • Camera frames exist only in the phone's memory during a scan and are destroyed when the scan ends, within seconds.
  • No face data is written to the phone's storage, sent to our servers, or shared with anyone.
  • If this ever changes, we will ask for your written consent first, update this policy, and destroy any such data once its purpose is met, and in no case later than three years after your last use of VitalKick.

09How long we keep information

  • Account and health data: while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your identifiable data from our active systems, except records the law requires us to keep, and it ages out of encrypted backups on their normal schedule.
  • Consent records and the access log: kept as long as the law requires, because they prove how your data was handled.
  • Data held for a healthcare organisation: kept and returned or destroyed as our agreement with that organisation requires.
  • Call bookings and texts: kept in Dr. Bizanti's calendar and phone until no longer needed for the conversation, or sooner if you ask.

10How we protect information

  • Health data is stored with a hosting provider that has signed a business associate agreement with us.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Database rules check your consent before any health data is saved, and limit each person to the data they are allowed to see.
  • Every read and write of health data is logged: who, what, when and from where.
  • Logs and crash reports are scrubbed of health information before they leave the app.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and download your data, from the app's settings
  • Correct details that are wrong
  • Delete your data and your account
  • See the access log of who opened your health data
  • Withdraw any consent, and opt out of marketing

Depending on your state, such as California, Washington, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Oregon, Texas or Nevada, the law may give you further rights, including the right to appeal if we turn down a request. You can use an authorised agent. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights. To make a request outside the app, call or text (305) 753-4452. We will confirm who you are before acting on it.

12Washington consumer health data

This section is our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act. It applies to Washington residents and anyone whose data is collected in Washington.

  • What we collect: the scan readings, things you log, and account and device details listed in "Information we collect".
  • Why: the purposes in "How we use information".
  • Where it comes from: you, the app on your phone, and organisations whose program you join.
  • Who receives it: the recipients in "How we share information". We do not sell consumer health data.
  • Consent: we ask separately to collect it and to share it, never inside these terms, and we never make the app depend on agreeing to share or sell.
  • Your rights: to confirm whether we collect, share or sell your data, to get a list of everyone we shared it with, to withdraw consent, and to have it deleted. Use the app's settings or call or text (305) 753-4452. You may appeal a refusal, and if the appeal is denied you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.

13Children

The VitalKick app is for adults 18 and over. This website is meant for adults and organisations and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, call or text us and we will delete it.

14Where information is stored

We store and process information in the United States. VitalKick is offered to people in the United States.

15Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use health data you already gave us, we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent again before the change applies to you.
