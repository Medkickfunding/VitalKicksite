02A wellness product, not medical care

VitalKick is a general wellness product. It helps you understand and follow your own readings and build healthier habits. It does not replace a visit, an examination or advice from a qualified clinician, and nothing in VitalKick is medical advice.

  • Readings are estimates from a camera. Light, movement, talking, makeup, skin conditions and your phone's camera can all affect them.
  • Hemoglobin and HbA1c are labelled as estimates. Only a blood test can confirm them.
  • VitalKick never tells you to begin, end or alter a medicine or a dose. Those decisions belong to you and your clinician.
  • If a reading crosses an urgent level, VitalKick suggests getting checked the same day. Please take that seriously.
  • If you think you are having an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not rely on VitalKick in an emergency.

Using VitalKick does not create a doctor-patient relationship with VitalKick or with Dr. Bizanti.
