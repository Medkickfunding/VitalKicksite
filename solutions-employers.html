A one-minute check people actually repeat.

Most wellness programs ask people to fill in forms once a year. VitalKick gives them something more interesting: their own vital signs in a minute, from their own phone, as often as they like, with habits that move the numbers.

Where it fits

How VitalKick works here.

01

Private by design

Each person's readings belong to them. Employers see participation and de-identified group trends, never an individual's readings.

02

Stress and recovery

Heart rate variability and stress level show how sleep, workload and rest show up in the body.

03

Habits that stick

Short, specific suggestions for movement, sleep and food, tied to the person's own numbers.

04

Group reports

Any group smaller than 20 people is never shown, so nobody can be singled out.

The readings that matter most

Twelve readings, these four first.

Every scan gives all twelve. For this setting, these are the ones people look at first.

Heart rate variabilityStress levelBlood pressurePulse rate

Readings are never used for decisions about employment, pay or insurance.

How a pilot runs

From first call to launch.

STEP 1

Talk

A 30-minute call about who you serve and what you want to learn. Book one here.

STEP 2

Scope

We agree the group, the length of the pilot and how success is measured, such as scans per week or how many people keep going.

STEP 3

Agree

A short pilot agreement, plus a business associate agreement wherever we handle health information for a provider.

STEP 4

Launch

People download the app, give their own consent, and scan. We review the results with you at the checkpoints we set together.

Guardrails

What we always do, and never do.

Wellness, not treatment

VitalKick supports conversations with clinicians. It never decides care, and never tells anyone to alter a medicine.

Consent from each person

Every participant agrees in the app to what is shared and with whom, and can withdraw in one tap.

Video stays on the phone

Only the computed numbers are stored. No face images or face geometry, ever.

Never sold

We never sell identifiable health data, and never profit from anything biometric.
